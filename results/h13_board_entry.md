NEGATIVES / CAVEATS (blunt)
 1. All routing outcomes, the competence tables, the keyword prior and the context effect (gain, 5% distraction) are simulated by me; "context helps only when relevant" is an assumption of the simulator. Effect sizes scale with those constants; only the SIGNS and the retrieval-variant ties are informative.
 2. Structural relevance = section / source-paragraph membership (h10): duplicate-ish paragraphs in other sections count as misses (understates every system equally); H queries share vocabulary with their section (favours lexical methods); absolute hit@1 ~.2 is not product accuracy.
 3. A confirm is not independent of A held (same disjoint pool, fresh draws); B (ruvnet + our docs) is the independent replication, has fewer queries (1029) and wider CIs: hit@1 and F1@3 hybrid gains do not pass the bootstrap Holm there.
 4. Post-hoc changes, all disclosed: (a) the vote-k parameter {1,3,5} was added to the tuned grid after a 3-seed smoke run with top-1 anchors showed 50% anchor accuracy (the smoke run was discarded); (b) the ctx-only gate and the Tqa world were added after the first full run to obtain a context-essential world; Tqa, the Tqa sweep and the vs-dense_s contrasts there are post-hoc and carry no verdict; (c) the global-ts baseline was added after the first full run showed ctl-irrel gains were pooling effects; the tuned picks are unchanged (tuning keeps the 6 pre-declared baselines) and the pre-change outputs are kept as results/h13_route_v1_6baselines.*; the vs-best-fair family is reported with both baseline sets; (d) rerank first-stage alpha .7, RRF k=60 and list depth 50 were fixed, not tuned.
 5. The tuned admission floors sit at the grid edge (.30 for most vote arms = almost no floor; .70 for ctx-only arms = almost never injects); a wider grid was not tried, so the ctx-only arms are likely under-using retrieval in T12-type worlds and the sweep shows the Tqa optimum at the low edge.
 6. Seeds resample 240 / 200 rows of one finite pool with fresh typo draws and simulation draws; t-CIs over seeds are narrow; for retrieval the cluster bootstrap is reported and required for "robust", for routing no query-cluster bootstrap exists (the A->B replication is the independence check).
 7. Latencies are single-thread micro-benchmarks on a loaded 16 GB box; the loop us/task excludes retrieval; MiniLM encode and spell corrector are not measured here.
 8. Retrieval top-lists were computed once (h13_top_*.bin) and the loop replays them; a live pipeline would add the embedding latency per task.
