# G16 (W17, retrieval track R, round 3): confidence-gated / adaptive-k / fresh / partitioned retrieval on the g1 hard-corpus fixtures

Files (all mine; src/*.rs untouched; no commits; no new crates/models/downloads): `src/bin/g16_route.rs`, `src/bin/g16_retrieval.rs`, `src/bin/g16_common/mod.rs` (world + policy engine), `py/g16_summary.py` (prints tables from the json), 
`results/g16_route.{json,log}` (tune seeds 1-8 on corpus A, held-out 31-60), `results/g16_route_confirm.{json,log}` (FROZEN picks, fresh corpus B, seeds 61-90), `results/g16_picks.json` (frozen configs + tuning log),
`results/g16_retrieval{,_confirm}.{json,log}`, `results/g16_retrieval_picks.json`, `results/g16_proposed_patch.md`; superseded intermediate runs kept for audit: `results/g16_route_v1..v5*.{log,json}` (see "What changed during the work").

HONESTY / CEILING. Everything is a seeded SIMULATION on top of the g1 fixtures (corpus A/B/C/D, MiniLM/BGE/tf-idf pair matrices). "Task success" = the g1_route d6-style world (hidden best agent per CONCEPT, w=.8, outcome feedback only). It measures routing success of a
knowledge layer, not any LLM quality. Retrieval makes the context better in recurring worlds; it does not discover anything. The worlds (below) were fixed before the policies were run; the tuning objective was changed once after the first full run (v1 -> v2, see below) and the
change made the policies MORE conservative, not less. No "10x"/novel-knowledge claim. **The headline goal (no Holm-significant loss in the control worlds while keeping the wins) is NOT met by any policy against the best fair baseline; it is met only against the SAME front router
without retrieval, only by the conservative variant, and only in some controls.** Details and the full trade-off frontier below.

## Worlds (src/bin/g16_common/mod.rs `worlds()`; stream is bit-identical to g1_route for the shared worlds)
sib16, rand16, sib64 (8 topics x 8 siblings), sib16-mixed50 (half the tasks are unrelated one-offs), plus new: sib16-weakid50 (with prob .5 the TEXT of a task comes from a random OTHER concept than the one that decides the agent = weak identification),
sib16-drift (the concept->best-agent table is re-drawn for all concepts at task 1000 = stale evidence), ctl-irrel (w=0: texts recur but carry no information), ctl-oneoff (w=0 and every text is an unrelated one-off).
Tuning panel (seeds 1-8): sib16, rand16, mixed50, weakid50, drift, ctl-irrel. **sib64 and ctl-oneoff are never tuned on** (independent worlds). Held-out seeds 31-60 on corpus A; confirm: frozen picks, corpus B (fresh texts/phrasings), seeds 61-90.
Policy = the g1_route `kn-direct+vote` neighbour-vote policy with a pluggable similarity. Reference arm R0 = exactly that (k5, thr .75, min_n 2, MiniLM(spell)+tf-idf hybrid .6/.4, global win-rate gate (6,.02,25)).
Fair baselines: keyword, eps-greedy, pooled-ts, hebbian-robust, warm(hebr), guarded(hebr); "best fair" is picked per world x metric on the held-out data (conservative); "same front" = guarded(hebr) (the router every vote policy sits on).

## What changed during the work (audit trail, no hiding)
v1 (results/g16_route_v1_noconstraint.*): tuning objective was the plain panel mean; every Post-confidence gate lost to R0 on the panel and all variants stayed -.012..-.025 in the controls. v2: constrained objective (above) + warm-front option + lenient-injection gate: R0 still won the panel.
Diagnosis (`--diag`): vote-played tasks in controls had pe .46 vs .75 -> winner's curse of a flat Beta(1,1) prior; v3: added hierarchical shrinkage (`kappa`); v4: shrinkage + the window gate (the combination, not either alone, beat R0 on the panel); v5: g1 window-gate parameter search (backoff etc., null); v6: gate grid widened (floor, delta, gamma) and the inject-gated variant added -> the FINAL above.
So the FINAL gate family was found by looking at held-out-adjacent failures of earlier arms on the TUNING panel logs and by the `--diag` run on held-out seeds 31-60 (mechanism only; nothing was tuned on 31-60 or 61-90). The frozen confirm on corpus B reproduced the qualitative picture (same signs for FINAL/G-post+g1 vs same-front in all 8 worlds; FINAL-R0 sib16 became Holm-significant, +.020).

## Caveats (be blunt)
- The worlds are mine; weak-id and drift are by-construction scenarios. Control "harm" magnitudes (.01-.03) are small in absolute terms and depend on the base skill model of sim::World (control worlds reach .75-.77 success, so a 1-pt relative loss is ~2 pts of regret).
- Same fixtures for corpus A and B (same 240 concepts / templates, different surface text): A->B is a text-instantiation replication, not new domains.
- Gates need tuning per similarity system; thr moves with the system (.45 mini raw, .75 hybrid). rand16 shows that the best similarity under the gate depends on the world (hybrid -.03 there).
- Seeds are resampled worlds, not independent corpora; CIs say nothing about other worlds. Holm families are large (hundreds-1000) so "ns" often means "underpowered", look at the CIs.
- E2's duplicates/revisions and E3a's second project are labels/sibling corpora, not real-world data.
- Latencies are single-thread microbenchmarks on a shared box.
