# lessons_h12 (R5-D, synthetic-until-validated)
- A zero-initialised, data-gated residual net on top of the incumbent (causal prequential-loss gate vs a token-additive predictor) is bit-identical to the incumbent when closed and wins only where the world has feature interactions (xor-cont/xor-tok +0.04..+0.21 vs best fair, held and confirm); it ties/loses by ~0.005-0.012 in keyword-prior worlds and controls (TOST +-0.01 fails in 2-4 of 7); not kept as a default, at most a bounded-loss opt-in for known-interaction streams.
- The causal gate is essential: the no-gate ablation loses -0.015..-0.055 in sim-no-traps/big/kw-drift4 and dips to 0.36 regret after drift.
- The neural residual does NOT help adaptation to drift: higher post-drift level in interaction worlds but a deeper dip (0.07-0.18 vs 0.02-0.04) and 2-4x slower recovery; in keyword drift worlds recovery equals or trails warm(hebr).
- Bandit over warm(hebr) hyper-parameter configs (all members learn from all outcomes): no Holm win vs best fair; small gains vs its own incumbent (+0.005..+0.026) in some worlds offset by -0.006..-0.012 in kw-drift4; 6x state, 4-7x ns. Meta-selector over {keyword, warm, safe, resid} inherits the residual's wins and pays an exploration tax of -0.004..-0.020 vs guarded-safe in keyword worlds.
- Any layer that explores or learns beyond the incumbent pays ~1% in worlds where the keyword rule is optimal (ctl-static): report that as bounded loss, never as harmless.
