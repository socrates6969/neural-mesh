NEGATIVES / CAVEATS (blunt):
 1. SYNTHETIC: templated g1 corpora written by us (30 topics x 8 sibling details, 3 surface forms per detail, 25% typo queries), worlds written by us. C2/D2 are new SEEDS of the same generator on the same 240 concept templates (text-instantiation replication, not new domains). The spell lever is large BECAUSE the corpus has 25% typo queries; without typos it would shrink (g1). Nothing here is evidence about real user text; synthetic-until-validated-on-real-traffic.
 2. RRF/RSF need an ABSOLUTE admission floor (a vote layer must be able to say "no neighbour"). I put it on the dense cosine, which makes RRF/RSF collapse onto the dense arm in practice (|d| <= .008 vs spell-only). Other floors (hybrid score, rank agreement, rank-gap) were NOT tried; RRF is rank-only by design, so this is a real limitation of using RRF for thresholded retrieval, not a proof RRF cannot work.
 3. The logistic reranker is trained with GROUND-TRUTH concept labels (corpus C); a deployment has no such labels (only graded outcomes), so its doc-level +.028 F1 is optimistic; and it still produced no end-to-end gain. 8 features, 48 bytes.
 4. Post-hoc changes: (a) raw-dense (no-spell) family added after pass 1 (the F-family Holm sizes grew; pass-1 files kept); (b) the STRICT "adds over spell" rule and the +-.01 non-inferiority margin are post-hoc (the margin is g16's); the literal pre-declared rule is reported unchanged; (c) ctl-noinfo is new and was declared before any policy ran, never tuned on; sib64/ctl-oneoff/ctl-noinfo are never tuned on.
 5. Gate regimes are g16's two arms, NOT re-tuned beyond (k, thr, min_n, fusion params, thr2); the 'safe' gate was tuned by g16 on A (mini-hybrid), so it is slightly favourable to the hybrid and slightly unfavourable to others; per-variant (k, thr, min_n) re-tuning covers that, the gate parameters did not move.
 6. Seeds are resampled worlds on a finite query pool, not independent corpora; Holm families have ~540 tests so "ns" often means underpowered (CIs are in the json). TOST/equivalence cells are reported, not claimed beyond +-.01.
 7. Single-thread micro latencies on a shared 16 GB box; the 2 us/call charge is a pessimistic placeholder (measured scorer cost is 0.13-0.21 us/call in memory); per-task routing-loop times include the world sim + front.
