We present OurNet and evaluate it against 5 baselines on 6 datasets in 2018. Code and data are available at release 3.

1. Methods
We trained all models with 103 epochs, a batch size of 256, a learning rate of 3e-4, and 4,333 participants (random seed 65; see Appendix B and [37]).

| LSTM-Base | 64.8 | 6.4 | 15.0 |
| T5-small | 69.9 | 8.6 | 15.1 |

ViT-B reaches 94.1 % in this setting (p < 0.04, n = 384).

3. Discussion
ResNet-50 outperforms DistilNet in terms of F1 [12].
On error rate, DistilNet improves on T5-small [4].
ResNet-50 outperforms OurNet in terms of error rate [25].
T5-small improves on OurNet in terms of F1 [31].
Prior work [36] used a single seed; our protocol follows [31].

We evaluate on 9 datasets only and did not test beyond 909 tokens; effects may not transfer to other regimes.
