NEGATIVES / CAVEATS (blunt):
 * Everything is conditional on MY synthetic edits and arrival model. If real revisions change content more (cos < .7) a cosine rule would be even worse and supersession just as good; if real stores carry <5 % stale rows the freshness gain shrinks proportionally (norev control = no gain, no harm). Real near-duplicates may be farther from their source than my edits (cos mean .99) which would lower the exact+near-merge savings.
 * Absolute retrieval levels are low (hit@1 .19-.21: 6.8k near-sibling paragraph fragments, section-title / cut-sentence queries) so +-.01 TOST is ~5 % of the level; margins in relative terms are wider than they look. Queries are clean (no typos / spell), relevance is structural (current version of the source paragraph), duplicates of a current paragraph count as relevant.
 * Latency/throughput of the tiers was NOT measured (matrix precomputation); dropped rows scale a brute-force scan linearly (h4), int8 software scans are slower per item (h4 1.5x) -- do not read byte savings as speed.
 * Value-eviction results depend on the synthetic Zipf popularity (and on usage queries being the same distribution as eval queries); the random-credit control shows the effect needs informative feedback.
 * Equivalence is a statement at +-0.01 hit@1 on 30 resampled streams; some EQ cells hide small significant losses (summary -.004..-.0075; cosfirst0.98 -.0011..-.0020 Holm-significant, flagged '-' in results/k1_combined.md).
 * Loop equivalence is low-powered (paired SD ~0.01-0.02): "not EQ" in the loop table means inconclusive, not harm.
 * Tuning touched only A tune seeds; the picks transferred to B (B's own tune would have picked cosfirst0.95 / cosnew0.80 -- not used). The cos grid extends to .70 and the soft-penalty grid hits its edge (8.0 = hard).
 * Post-hoc (declared): `supdel-arrival`, controls norev/hirev/nodup/val25rand (added after the main stages, run on confirm seeds only), the half-credit stale sensitivity was declared before the runs.
