# K1 PRE-DECLARATION (written after the tune stage, BEFORE any held-out (31-60) or confirm (61-90) run)

Honest framing: this concerns the knowledge ACCESS / MEMORY layer (what we keep, how big it is, whether the freshest version wins); it adds no knowledge to the frozen model.
Everything below is SYNTHETIC-until-validated: REAL = paragraphs, structure (src/file/ordinal keys, H-sections), queries, MiniLM vectors, BM25 tokens, topic clusters; SYNTHETIC = edited revisions, near-duplicates, exact re-ingestion, noise records, arrival order, query popularity, every closed-loop outcome.

## Worlds / data (fixed before any policy ran)
* World A = 6831 real paragraphs (709 crate groups), world B = 4181 (ruvnet + this repo's prose); saved h10 fixtures (md5 results/h10_corpus.md5), B is NOT rebuilt from live files.
* py/k1_prep.py adds OLD versions (30% of units are candidates; per seed each candidate unit is active w.p. .7; 1/3 of candidates have a 2-deep chain; 10% of old records arrive LATE, after the new version), NEAR-duplicates (20% of paragraphs, 1 or 2 variants, active w.p. .75), exact re-ingestions (12% of paragraphs, 1-3 copies), NOISE (30% of N, real paragraphs of the other world, active .8). Edits are synthetic (word substitution 15/30%, sentence splice/trim/extend); cosine(old, current) mean .82-.83 (p5 .54, p95 .98); cosine(near-dup, current) mean .986-.990 (p5 .95-.97). The overlap of the upper tail of old and the lower tail of near-dup is real and disclosed: it decides how well any cosine rule can separate revisions from duplicates.
* Stream: units arrive at a random time, old before new (except 10% late), 8 epochs; evaluation at 25/50/75/100% of the stream. Queries = the h10 pool (H section titles, S held-out sentences, clean text, no typos), asked only once ALL relevant paragraphs' current versions have arrived. Relevance = the CURRENT version (or an exact/near copy of it) of the source paragraph/section; a superseded version is never relevant.
* Retrieval: z-score fusion .7 dense (MiniLM cosine) + .3 BM25 (h13 frozen), top-10, ties by row order. Dense scores per tier: f32 / block-int16 (flatq BlockFp16, 816 B) / int8 (388 B); verified identical to flatq::FlatIndex (`k1_growth check`: 200/200 top-5 ids, max score diff 6.6e-7).

## Metrics (pre-declared)
* PRIMARY quality: hit@1 and MRR@10 at the FINAL checkpoint on 400 uniformly drawn eligible queries (paired across arms: same stream, same queries). Secondary: P@3, distinct-recall@3, popularity-weighted versions (Zipf s=.8, 400 queries), revised-key subset, H vs S, noise@1.
* STALENESS = share of top-1 results whose content is a superseded version of its structural key (key has a later-arrived revision); also `store_stale_frac` (share of retained rows that are superseded) and the revised-key subset.
* BYTES = dense (tier row bytes) + 24 B metadata/row + sparse BM25 postings (8 B per unique term); reported total and dense-only.
* CLOSED LOOP: g16/h13 engine (copied) with the frozen h13 hybz_s picks (safe gate: thr .3, min_n 2, k 5; g1 gate from results/h13_route_picks.json), front guarded(hebr), 2000 tasks drawn from the pool, worlds {T12, T12-drift, Tctx, Tqa (context-essential), ctl-irrel (context never helps)}; the injected top-1 paragraph is relevant iff structurally the current version of the source; a stale version of the right key is treated as IRRELEVANT (p*0.95) in the primary, as half credit in the declared sensitivity `safe_s50`. Oracle and front-only references reported.

## Statistics (pre-declared)
* Seeds: tune 1-8 (free hyper-parameters only), held-out 31-60, confirm 61-90 (frozen). Picks are tuned ONLY on world A seeds 1-8 and applied unchanged to world A held/confirm AND to world B held/confirm (B is the independent text world; B's own tune output is reported only for comparison).
* Paired t-CI over seeds vs `none` (no compaction); TOST equivalence margin +-0.01 (absolute) on hit@1, MRR and closed-loop H2000; Holm over the frozen-arm family (frozen arms x {hit@1, MRR}) and, separately, over frozen arms x 5 worlds x 2 gates for the loop. EQ claim only if EQ (Holm-adjusted TOST p <= .05) in BOTH held and confirm. NI (non-inferior) = lower 95% CI bound > -0.01 (descriptive). A "gain" needs the Holm-adjusted paired p < .05 in both stages.
* Pick rules (tune seeds only): cosfirst/cosnew = most aggressive theta (.70 ... .98) with tune dhit1 >= -0.005 AND dMRR >= -0.005 (else .98); supsoft lambda and recency beta = argmax hit@1+MRR. All grid points of every family are still run and reported (they are descriptive; only the frozen picks + fixed arms enter the Holm family).
* Arms: none | exact (src/dedup.rs Deduper default) | cosfirst/cosnew theta grid | supdel (structural-key supersession by deletion, revision stamps decide, stale arrivals dropped, equal revision = merge with count) | supsoft lambda grid (keep rows, penalise superseded in z units) | rec beta grid (generic recency weight, no structural info) | fifo50/25, lru50/25, val50/25 (hard byte cap = fraction of the unbounded store's final bytes, value = decayed count of top-1 relevant usage credits, 200 usage queries/epoch drawn from the same Zipf popularity) | tier-f16 / tier-int8 / tier.2/.3 / tier.1/.2 (hot f32 / warm block-fp16 / cold int8 by arrival age) | summary (anchor rows per structural section, members' vectors dropped, text/BM25 kept; paragraph = best-BM25 member) | stacks (exact+supdel, supdel+fifo25, supdel+val25, supdel+summary, exact+supdel+tier.2/.3, exact+supdel+cosfirst0.95).
* No post-hoc arm additions after the tune stage; anything added later is labelled POST-HOC.
