mechanisms (all inside the bin; SonaB re-implements Guarded<Hebbian> because the guard counts are private; default == frozen x1 guarded(hebr), asserted bit-identical):
 EXPLORATION: (expl) while the guard follows the keyword rule, with prob eps try the challenger with the highest Beta-UCB, only if its UCB reaches the keyword agent's LCB (uncertainty-directed), at most `budget` times per pattern (per-pattern annealed budget, refilled on a drift alarm), optional pattern prior Beta(1+lam*m_a, 1+lam*(1-m_a)) from the agent's global success (optimistic init from pattern priors); (ts) Thompson sampling over the pattern-conditioned posteriors (guard counts pooled over the task's features, count scale, optimistic pseudo-successes for agents with <3 outcomes, same pattern prior) replacing Hebbian after hand-over.
 FORGETTING: (tdecay) lazy exponential decay of a pattern row by elapsed tasks (per-pattern recency); (protect) EWC-like: per-update discount weakened on rows whose best agent is proven good, plasticity kept elsewhere; (detect) CUSUM on the chosen agent's pattern cell (downward shift), on alarm the row's evidence is scaled by `keep` and the exploration budget refilled (drift-detection reset). (full) = best subset of {expl, ts, forgetting parts} by the tuning objective (rt/no-mechanism included, so full >= rt on tune seeds; it selected detect alone in both modes).

NEGATIVES / CAVEATS (read with the claim)
 (1) the goal "improve SonaPlus beyond the best fair baseline" is NOT met; ties at best. D7's conclusion (SonaPlus' SONA-specific parts add nothing) is extended: exploration, Thompson sampling, pattern priors, recency/importance decay, EWC-like protection and drift-reset add nothing measurable over guarded(hebr)+reuse in these worlds.
 (2) worlds (three drift generators: one reshuffle of half the topics / ramp / recurrent), the weak-observation model and the reuse channel are author-built (x1/x3/d7 generators + my three drift worlds); one drift magnitude; no real traffic. Real drift may be rarer, noisier or more structured; nothing here transfers without validation.
 (3) `rt`, the in-family "ablation without the mechanism", tuned to discount 1.0 in both modes (stationary worlds dominate the objective), so detect's +0.02..+0.05 over rt in drift worlds is largely "regaining plasticity"; the external comparators (warm(hebr), hebbian-robust, guarded-safe with discount .97) are stronger there. Likewise the 61-90 tdecay/detect wins over guarded-safe in traps(main) are the retuned guard, not the mechanism.
 (4) round 2 is post-hoc (see above); counted as a separate experiment, not a rescue. Round-1 grids were written before any run; round 1 and 2 share the same frozen worlds and seeds protocol.
 (5) ONE config per mechanism over ALL worlds (controls included) by design; per-world retuning would improve individual cells but a deployed router cannot know its world. Grid sizes: rt 72 (normal) / 216 (weak), expl 36, ts 36, tdecay 12, protect 18, detect 24 (96 in round 2), 32 combos.
 (6) Holm families are per (mode x comparator) over 270 tests; the best baseline per (world,H) is picked on evaluation seeds (favours the baselines). The Beta sampler is my own Marsaglia-Tsang (seeded, deterministic). 31-60 and 61-90 agree except where noted. 8 tuning seeds only.
