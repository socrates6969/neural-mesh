## RESULT (plain)
**The success criterion is NOT met by any arm, in either text world.** Criterion = Holm-significant wins vs best fair baseline in T12, T12-drift, T48 AND no Holm loss (or TOST +-.01) vs best fair baseline in ctl-irrel, ctl-oneoff, ctl-noinfo, in text world A and B (frozen confirm 61-90). The frontier is clean and has one cause:
1. **Retrieval is not what harms the controls; the FRONT is.** On the SAME front, the retrieval layer (g1: inject the retrieved anchor as a feature + window-gated vote) is harmless-to-positive in the controls (g1|meta3 minus meta3-front, H2000, A conf | B conf: ctl-irrel -.003 | -.001, ctl-oneoff +.020* | +.013, ctl-noinfo +.009 | +.002) and +.016..+.037 in the dependent worlds (A conf T12 +.035*, drift +.037*, T48 +.016*; B conf +.026*, +.025*, +.010 n.s.). The deficit against the BEST FAIR baseline in the topic-independent controls (global-ts, .65 in ctl-irrel, .52-.53 in ctl-noinfo) comes from the guarded/warm fronts being topic-keyed (slow in a world where topic carries no information): g1|warm is -.053 / -.060 below global-ts in ctl-irrel (A | B conf) and -.036 / -.032 in ctl-noinfo (all Holm losses), although it is +.09 / +.08 and +.024 above the old 6-baseline best fair there.
2. **A meta-front fixes the controls at a price that eats most of the dependent-world gain.** Thompson sampling over complete pipelines {warm+retrieval, global-ts(+retrieval)} (P2w, P2w+) gets ctl-irrel -.004 / -.003 vs global-ts (TOST-equivalent +-.01 for P2w+ in A conf AND B conf), ctl-oneoff +.011 / +.015*, ctl-noinfo -.012! / -.011 (A: Holm loss; B: not significant), while T12 +.023* / +.017*, T12-drift +.027* / +.022*, T48 -.006 / -.002 (n.s.). Measured price of choosing the front online vs the oracle front per world (g1|warm in dependent worlds + oneoff, global-ts in irrel/noinfo; mean of the 3 splits): dependent worlds -.004..-.019, controls -.003..-.016. No pipeline-meta variant wins T48 (best +.007 held, -.009 A conf, -.015 B conf for P2w).
3. **Confidence gates on the vote (g16 post gate, shrinkage, anchor-local Bayes test, online A/B holdout) do not help; ungated g1 is better.** They keep only ~15-40% of the g1 retrieval value over the same meta-front in dependent worlds (T12 A conf) (A conf T12 vs meta3-front: g1 +.035*, safe +.006, shrink +.009, local +.008, A/B +.014) and do not remove the control deficit (shrink|meta3 ctl-irrel -.020!, -.013!, -.018! in A conf; local -.012!/-.010!/-.012!), because the deficit is the front, not the vote. h13's "g16's gate is required" is again NOT reproduced on this world: the ungated arm is not harmful on the same front.
4. **POST-HOC finding (ablation run after the frozen confirm, NO verdict, needs fresh seeds):** the VOTE is not what carries the gain. Feeding the retrieved anchor to the front as a plain feature and never voting ("feat|warm") beats the g1 arm: vs BF7 (A conf | B conf) T12 +.042* | +.034*, T12-drift +.041* | +.033*, T48 +.017* | +.011 (n.s.), ctl-oneoff +.025* | +.030*; by level above the old 6-baseline best fair in all three controls in both worlds (not Holm-tested in the primary family, appended post hoc). Turning context injection off (injection precision of the real top-1 paragraph is .23-.35, below the ~.57 break-even of the simulator's 15%-gain / 5%-distraction model) adds +.000..+.008 on top. Warm front instead of guarded front: g1|warm - g1|guard +.010 | +.015 (T12), +.010 | +.014 (drift) (A conf | B conf; confounded with that pick's ctx-off, with ctx on +.006..+.013).

Best fair picked per world on the evaluated seeds (conservative).
Fronts: guarded(hebr) | warm(hebr) | global-ts | META: Thompson sampling over fronts (front-level, credit on non-vote tasks) or over complete pipelines (pipeline-level, credit on all tasks); all fronts are trained on every outcome. Gates: g1 (h13: window gate, inject kn:<anchor> always), safe (h13 G-post+g1), shrink (g16 FINAL: anchor evidence shrunk to the agent's global rate, Beta posterior vs front reference, window gate, inject only when open), local (per-anchor Bayes test p_vote > p_front-in-this-anchor with the front's rate shrunk to the global front rate, normal approximation of the Beta difference, asymmetric-loss threshold gamma), A/B wrapper (randomised eps share of gate-eligible tasks withholds the layer, layer off when P(effect>0) < gamma_ab, measurement continues while off, 50% burn-in split), injection gate (inject the paragraph only if top-1 dense cosine >= inj_thr; 2.0 = never).
Tuning: A seeds 1-8 on {T12, T12-drift, Tctx, ctl-irrel, ctl-noinfo}; objective = mean_treat (H500+H2000)/2 - 2 x mean_ctl max(0, BF7 - v - .003) (BF7 now contains global-ts, so guard-fronted arms are penalised by construction); never tuned on T48, T12-noctx, Tqa, ctl-oneoff. Stage 1: META params (front-level) on `meta3-front`; stage 1b: pipeline arms (META n0 {10,30}, thr {.3,.5}, k {3,5}, inj_thr {0,.6,.7,.8,2}); stage 2: each new gate with META frozen. Frozen picks in `results/k5_picks_frozen.json` (md5 in `k5_frozen.md5`). Held 31-60 (A), frozen confirm A 61-90 and B 61-90 (independent text). ## What survives / what does not
SURVIVES: (a) retrieval-anchored routing helps the dependent worlds on every front (+.01..+.04 vs the same front, both text worlds), (b) it is harmless on the SAME front in all three controls incl. the new ctl-noinfo, (c) once global-ts is part of the fair set, the whole control deficit is the front (A conf ctl-irrel g1|warm -.053), (d) a pipeline-level Thompson meta over {topic-keyed+retrieval, global-ts} brings ctl-irrel to TOST-equivalence (P2w+, P2g) at a dependent-world price of ~.01-.02, (e) h13's observation that the ungated layer needs no gate is confirmed on the new control too.
DOES NOT: (a) any arm passing the full criterion in both worlds (best compromise P2w+: 2/3 dependent wins in both worlds, 0 Holm control losses in B but -.012! in ctl-noinfo in A); (b) any confidence gate (post / shrinkage / local Bayes / A/B) improving on ungated g1; (c) T48 in the independent text world B (even the ungated g1|warm is only +.003 there, 48 anchors over a smaller store); (d) front selection by a cleverer estimator without exploration.

## NEGATIVES / CAVEATS (blunt)
1. All outcomes and the context effect are simulated; effect sizes scale with those constants (gain .15, distraction .05, agent tables); only signs and rankings are informative. The finding that context injection is net negative at the real top-1 precision is a CONSEQUENCE of the assumed 5% distraction vs 15% gain, not a measurement.
2. ctl-noinfo is my new control; best fair there is global-ts (.52-.53); a loss of .01-.012 is Holm-significant with 30 paired seeds because the paired sd is small. The criterion is therefore a sharp one for any bandit-based front. A looser margin (TOST +-.015) would flip several verdicts; the margin was declared +-.01 beforehand and was not changed.
3. BF7 contains global-ts only because h13 added it post hoc and the brief made it the reference; relative to the 6 pre-h13 baselines the same arms win the controls by +.04..+.14. Both are reported; neither is hidden.
4. `meta3-front` (adaptive front without retrieval) is a new fair-ish arm: against BF8 (best of BF7 and it) results are identical to BF7 for all retrieval arms (it never is the best fair baseline in a criterion world).
5. Tuning picks come from 8 seeds and several are on grid edges (thr .3, k 5, META disc 1.0); picks varied between v1/v2/v3 reruns of the tuner within +-.005 objective: the arms' relative order inside the meta family is within noise, only the large contrasts (feature-vs-vote, gates vs ungated, meta vs fixed front) are robust.
6. Holm families are large (PRIMARY 60 tests per reference; all-arms ~600) and 30 seeds are resampled worlds, not independent corpora; A->B is a text-instantiation replication. No real traffic; no LLM reads the paragraph; every retrieval top list was precomputed once (a live loop adds the embedding latency per task).
7. Cost: the layer needs ONE retrieval per task (1 query embedding + ~1.7 ms brute-force scan on 6.8k paragraphs, measured by h13) plus the loop (measured us/task, A conf T12: guarded 3.8, warm 4.5, global-ts 2.0, g1|warm 4.9, P2w 5.9, P2w+ 7.6, g1|meta3 9.9, meta3-front 7.4; noisy shared box); the meta runs all fronts' updates every task; no extra model/verifier calls; the A/B wrapper withholds the layer on a measured 10-55% of tasks.
