# lessons h20 (R9-D role/persona routing + domain-corpus retrieval) -- synthetic-until-validated
- Persona routing's value is the assumed role bonus times role-detection accuracy minus mismatch penalty (G1-mod anchor +.025/+.031 held/confirm at b=.05, acc .83; ctl oracle +.050 = b); it is exactly 0 when the role does nothing and -.03..-.06 when it hurts. Agent routing headroom (~.2) is 4-10x the whole persona effect.
- Learned role routing from graded outcomes (11-arm keyword bandit) loses (-.01..-.03) at b=.05 vs noise .10: the signal is too small to learn through many arms in 1000 tasks. The online randomised A/B confidence gate is the only role policy that stays within +-.01 in zero/harm worlds, but it captures only ~25% of a real +.03 effect inside 1000 tasks (fires on 11-23% of calls): sample size, not design, is the limit. Per-agent gating adds nothing even with heterogeneous responsiveness.
- Domain-prioritised pooled retrieval (kNN-vote domain prior, lam 16) gives +.010..+.021 e2e on two REAL doc corpora ONLY through the assumed off-domain-context harm (purity 27%->13%, 41%->21% off-domain); recall (hit@5) and MRR are flat to slightly negative (confusable queries -.02..-.05 hit@5). With harm H=0 the e2e gain is +.002/-.003/0. Oracle domain labels would give +.04..+.08 and the router realises ~25-40% of it, none of the recall gain.
- Templated/synthetic role corpora (syn10: domain words in the query, unique entities) have no headroom for domain partitioning (ties, TOST-equivalent), the g1 lesson again; real text shows the only (purity) effect.
- A label-structure self-check (dense-kNN label purity excess >= .15) is what makes the domain prior harmless on no-structure controls (identical to pooled); without it the same arm loses hit@5 -.07..-.11. A guard that returns the baseline is a by-construction tie, not evidence of router robustness.
- Practical hygiene: share RNG streams carefully across arms (a zero-effect arm that consumes the agent-exploration stream produced a spurious +.02 until the streams were separated); effect worlds that share agents/tasks per seed are not independent samples.
