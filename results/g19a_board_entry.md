## G19-A (W20, SONA-A): can SonaPlus be improved by better pattern-memory KEYING and RETRIEVAL?

All numbers are SYNTHETIC-until-validated-on-real-traffic (author-built generators). No claim of time dilation, neuromorphic breakthrough, 10x-on-same-compute or novel intelligence.
Code: src/bin/g19_sona_a.rs + src/bin/g19_common/{mod.rs,keyed.rs} (src/*.rs untouched). Proposals: results/g19a_proposed_patch.md.

Protocol: tune seeds 1-8 only (regret-averse objective vs the best of the 6 spec baselines + retuned guarded, per world x horizon, staged grids), held-out 31-60, fresh confirm 61-90 (`--held 61-90`, SAME frozen tuning because tuning always reruns on seeds 1-8 and is deterministic), plus one extra fresh block 91-120 for a post-hoc variant. Paired 95% t-CI, Holm per family. Baselines (all in every normal-feedback family): keyword, eps-greedy, pooled-ts, hebbian-robust, warm(hebr), guarded-safe (= defaults::recommended_router, frozen), plus guarded-rt (guard retuned on 1-8 over the same grid size as the candidate). Best baseline is picked post hoc per world x H on the held-out mean (conservative).

Process honesty (rounds; earlier round logs are kept as results/g19a_round{1,2,3,4}_31-60.* but were produced by earlier code states and are NOT reproducible by the final binary):
  * round 1: 10 worlds fixed before any policy ran (5 new + 5 d7 controls), guard tuned jointly. Result: K lost to guarded-safe nearly everywhere; in conj-* the keyword guard hid the learner.
  * round 2: ADDED worlds conjT-clean/noisy (trap keyword so the learner decides) and var-wide-traps; anchors made significance-aware (Poisson-tail). Guard tuning made K harmful in controls (correct-keywords/sim-notraps).
  * round 3: guard FROZEN to defaults::recommended_guard() for every K arm (new mechanism cannot change when the keyword rule is followed). Pattern memory (ungated) still cost 0.3-1.4 pts in controls.
  * round 4: added the EVIDENCE GATE (hybrid with hebbian-robust). Controls became harmless.
  * round 5 (final): added shortmod-traps/clean (conjunction structure from a DIFFERENT generator) and weak-shortmod-traps; weak-mode anchor thresholds tuned too. Final runs below. The worlds added after round 1 were added after seeing results; 61-90 and 91-120 are untouched by those decisions only as far as seeds are concerned (the worlds/mechanism were designed with 31-60 in view). min_w=8 (see below) is a POST-HOC one-factor choice made after the 31-60 sensitivity table.

### VERIFY REQUEST — W20 — SONA-A learned anchor keys (online PMI-lift feature clustering) -- RESULT: NEUTRAL in normal feedback, modest +ve in sparse feedback; needs permissive thresholds that cost late-horizon in controls
rerun: `--diag-anchors` (policy-free, fast): $CARGO_TARGET_DIR/release/g19_sona_a.exe --diag-anchors > results/g19a_diag_anchors.log ; sensitivity: `--sens` / `--sens --held 61-90` (see block above).
mechanism: Anchors (keyed.rs) merge-only agglomeration from the task history: token pairs with count>=3, lift P(a,b)/(P(a)P(b))>=lift_min and Poisson upper tail <= alpha are unioned (repeated at cluster level until a fixpoint, every 25 tasks); guard counts and memory statistics migrate on a merge. ORACLE topic anchors are a diagnostic only.
numbers: clustering quality (5 seeds): purity 1.00 in every world; with permissive thresholds (lift 3, alpha 1e-2) topics are recovered by t=500 (var-traps 0.98 of features merged, 8.0 clusters of 8) and t=250 in 3-form worlds; the tuned conservative thresholds (lift 8, alpha 1e-4) merge only 20%/55%/65% of var-traps features at t=500/1000/2000 and 15% in traps(main).
  * normal feedback: K-full - K[no anchors] direct 1 W / 0 L (31-60), 3 W / 1 L (61-90); K[anchors+hebbian] == guarded-safe. Permissive anchors give EARLY gains (vs guarded-safe at H500: var-traps +0.0116 [sig], shortmod-traps +0.0485 vs +0.0197 tuned, shortmod-clean +0.0369 vs +0.0093) but cost at H2000 in controls (traps(main) -0.0050, sim-random -0.0060, sim-notraps -0.0040, correct-keywords -0.0023; 31-60) -> the objective rejected them.
  * ORACLE anchors vs K-full: 24 W / 2 L (31-60), 26 W / 2 L: var-single(ctl) +0.195 (H500) / +0.114 (H2000), var-traps +0.043 (H500) -> headroom exists for better (semantic/embedding) anchors than co-occurrence; the repo's knowledge.rs anchors need real embeddings and were NOT tested here (hash embeddings of unrelated synonym strings carry no similarity).
  * sparse (weak, same reuse signal for every arm): K-full[reuse] - K[no anchors][reuse] 4 W / 0 L in both blocks (31-60 H2000: weak-var-traps +0.0277, weak-var-wide-traps +0.0170, weak-shortmod-traps +0.0280). Anchors pool the scarce delayed signal across surface forms.
negatives: anchors never changed the sign of any normal-feedback comparison; Hebbian rows are NOT migrated on a merge (limitation); a wrong early merge would be irreversible (never observed: purity 1.00 on these generators; real noisy features unknown).

### VERIFY REQUEST — W20 — SONA-A pattern consolidation (capacity-bounded store, EWC-style importance merge) -- RESULT: importance-merge beats LRU/FIFO/count/drop at equal capacity; none beats uncapped
rerun: part of the main run (memory family; world memT-conj48 = 12 topics x 4 modifiers x trap keywords x noise, 1161 distinct patterns uncapped on seed 31).
mechanism: importance = evidence x (best agent mean - mean over agents) of a pattern; when the store exceeds `cap` the least important pattern is merged into its nearest (Jaccard) pattern with similarity-weighted counts (consolidation), vs dropped, LRU, FIFO, count-drop.
numbers (31-60, H2000 mean expected success; guarded-safe 0.4765, guarded-rt 0.5001, eps-greedy 0.5306): K-full uncapped 0.5325 (1161 patterns); cap32 importance-merge 0.5115, cap16 0.5018 (16 patterns, 1880 evictions), cap8 0.4925; cap16 importance-drop 0.4886, count-drop 0.4928, LRU 0.4762, FIFO 0.4785 (LRU/FIFO keep none of the benefit). Direct (Holm wins/losses of 5 cells) cap16 importance-merge minus importance-drop / LRU / FIFO / count-drop: 2/0, 2/0, 2/0, 5/0 (31-60) and 2/0 each (61-90); cap32 importance-merge minus drop / LRU / FIFO / count-drop: 3/0, 3/0, 2/0, 1/0 (31-60) and 3/0 each (61-90); importance-merge minus uncapped: 0 wins, 1-3 losses at each cap (it loses to the uncapped store). Log lines print 'mem-conj48 seed 31 ...' but the world is memT-conj48.
negatives: single world designed to stress capacity; memory reduction is a cost-saving, NOT a quality gain over uncapped; K-full uncapped beats the best baseline in memT only marginally at H2000 (0.5325 vs eps-greedy 0.5306).

### VERIFY REQUEST — W20 — SONA-A trajectory-level credit (session worlds: only "all 3 steps ok" is reported at session end) -- RESULT: big generic gain over broadcast for EVERY router; the SONA pattern-memory estimator is WORSE than a plain per-feature estimator
rerun: part of the main run (session family; worlds sess-traps, sess-var-traps, sess-sim-random).
mechanism: Credited wrapper: on a failed session, step i is credited 1 - q_i with q_i = (1-p_i)/(1-prod p_j) (posterior that step i failed given the session failed), success credits every step 1; p_i from (a) the policy's own estimate (pattern memory) or (b) a plain per-feature mean estimator ("uniest"). Baselines: broadcast of the shared bit (what a session-level hook gives), and every baseline ALSO fed credit-uniest (strict pool).
numbers (H2000, 31-60): guarded-safe broadcast -> credit-uniest: sess-traps 0.5351 -> 0.7120, sess-var-traps 0.5002 -> 0.6894, sess-sim-random 0.5623 -> 0.7400; K-full[credit-own] - K-full[broadcast] 15 W / 0 L in both blocks. K-full[credit-own] - K-full[credit-uniest]: 0 W / 12 L (31-60), 0 W / 10 L (61-90); K-full[credit-own] - guarded-rt[credit-uniest]: 0 W / 7 L. Strict pool (broadcast + credit-fed baselines): no K arm wins a cell (0 wins; 3-15 losses per arm); the best arms are guarded-safe[credit-uniest] / K-full[credit-uniest] (tied: sess-traps 0.7120 vs 0.7081).
negatives: the credit gain is a property of the wrapper (it helps every router), so it is NOT a SONA-specific improvement; the series-system (all-must-succeed) observation model is author-built.

### SUMMARY (plain)
SonaPlus CAN be improved by set-level (conjunction) pattern keys, gated by evidence and sitting behind the unchanged recommended guard: on the 5 worlds with conjunction structure it beats the best of {keyword, eps-greedy, pooled-ts, hebbian-robust, warm(hebr), guarded-safe} at Holm-significance in held-out 31-60, fresh 61-90 (and 91-120 for min_w=8) by +0.02..+0.07 at H2000, with the ablation (same guard + anchors, no pattern memory; and per-feature pattern memory) showing 0 wins -- i.e. the SET-LEVEL key is the part that helps, and the reuse signal is not involved in those normal-feedback worlds. It is harmless in the 6 control worlds (no Holm loss vs the best baseline) but costs 0.5-1.7 points in additive many-surface-form worlds, and the gain depends entirely on author-built conjunction worlds. Learned anchor keys, kNN blending, consolidation and trajectory credit with the SONA estimator add nothing measurable over the simpler alternatives in the normal-feedback setting (anchors help only under sparse feedback; importance-merge only saves memory vs LRU; credit helps every router equally).
