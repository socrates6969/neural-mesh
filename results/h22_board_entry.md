## Results (full-stream mean expected success; diff vs BEST FAIR baseline = best of 16 baseline arms: 6 routers x {tokens, +anc32, +anc128} anchor tokens; Holm; held 31-60 / confirm 61-90, same frozen tuning from seeds 1-8)
1. g12 REPRODUCES: all 120 cells x 8 arms (6 baselines, ann, ann+kw) match results/g12_annrouter*.json to 0e0 in both splits.
2. Existing keyword worlds (5 worlds, T 2000/6000/20000): g12 `ann` loses everywhere (-.02..-.19). New cascade+kw `casc` closes most of the gap but still loses: low -.020..-.035, mid -.001..-.024, high +.003..-.015 (TOST +-.01 passes in ~4/30 cells; 15/30 Holm LOSS). Tasks-to-parity vs late-best fair: sim-fixed/random 500 (held) but 10500 (confirm), no-traps 11.5-15.5k, big 18.5k/never. Bounded loss, never a win.
3. Real-shaped worlds (12 = 2 text sources x clu12-6ag, clu48-32ag, smlin-6ag, smrbf-8ag, smrbf-drift-8ag, control shuffled):
 - MLP(emb) vs best fair: LOSS in 9-10/12 at T=2000/6000 (up to -.13 clu48, -.08 smrbf); at 20000 ~parity in clu12 (-.003/-.012) and Holm WIN only in smrbf-drift (+.008/+.012 held, +.011/+.010 confirm) and tiny control +.003..+.006. vs best anchor-Hebbian: same except smrbf ties (-.003).
 - Equal-info ablations: MLP without embedding (tokens only) is far worse (clu12 .558 vs .678 at 20000); MLP given tokens+anchor but no embedding .633. So the embedding helps the MLP, but anchor-Hebbian uses it just as well.
 - Cascade net `casc(emb)` (tuned: raw384 + keyword skip): T=2000 loses/ties; T=6000 WINS in A-smlin (+.013/+.011), A-smrbf-drift (+.006/+.010) in both splits (B-clu12 +.007 held only, B-drift +.009 held only); T=20000 WINS in 6/12 worlds in both splits (clu12 +.015 A,B; smlin +.035/+.024; drift +.016/+.017; vs anchor-Hebbian also smrbf +.009/+.007), LOSES clu48-32ag (-.03..-.05), smrbf vs keyword@tok (-.02..-.026), and the no-information control (-.007..-.009, Holm LOSS). Crossover (curves, T=20000 run): 500-1500 tasks vs late-best baseline in clu12/smlin/drift (late-window metric), ~3500-5000 vs anchor-Hebbian in smrbf, 17k in B-clu48, never in A-clu48; full-stream crossover between 2000 and 6000.
 - IMPORTANT: the gain is the LINEAR head, not hidden units: casc-linear-head (hmax 0) at T=20000: A-clu12 .7076 > casc .6960 > MLP .6779 (same ordering in all 10 informative worlds, both splits; not a pre-declared test).
4. Keep rule: MLP -> NOT kept (wins in 1 family only, loses keyword worlds by up to -.19 for g12 ann). Cascade/linear-embedding head -> wins >1 real-shaped world (2 replicated at 6000, 6 at 20000) but is NOT harmless: keyword worlds bounded loss -.004..-.035, control -.007..-.009 at 20000, fails 32-agent/RBF worlds. Only a possible opt-in "linear head on embeddings" for >=6000-task, <=8-agent pools that already compute embeddings. No src patch proposed. Caveats: smooth worlds' keyword prior is informed (strong baseline early); worlds sample sentences with replacement (B at 20000 repeats each sentence ~4x); anchors are offline k-means (idealised); cascade tuned over 96 configs vs baselines 28 per variant; tuning on seeds 1-8 only; competence functions are mine (smlin is linear in random projections by construction).
