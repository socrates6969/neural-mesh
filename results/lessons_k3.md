# lessons_k3 (R10+ knowledge batch; extraction + verification on REAL tables / Cargo.toml / changelogs; synthetic renderings + assumed reader noise)
- (k3) Real crate tables break unit semantics, not layout: a dev-trained extractor gets F1 0.99-1.00 on repo tables but 0.75 on vendored-crate tables (lenient 1.000: every error is a unit/scale error: ns/us/MiB/s/KB/min/km, micro sign vs Greek mu). 64% of crate cells carry a unit/suffix/% marker. Layout (pipe/ws/csv/wrap) moves F1 by <0.03.
- (k3) Verification is worth much more on real tables than in h19's generated corpora when the verifier knows more than the extractor: v1 dev-only extractor unverified is WORSE than the plain reader on crates (-0.037, 11.6% silent wrong answers, 9 of 126 tests non-significant under global Holm), +0.046 with a broad independent verifier, +0.068 with its repairs (h19: +0.010..0.014). A verifier restricted to the extractor's own knowledge adds exactly 0.000 (catch 0.000 vs 0.994); a verifier that knows LESS than the extractor false-flags 26% of correct facts.
- (k3) h19's phrasing collapse transfers: cue/regex extractors 0.97-1.00 on the families they were built on -> 0.00-0.23 on held-out families (tables and Cargo.toml/changelog alike). Typed shape scanners hold (files 0.98 -> 0.92); generic prose-template induction (tuned only on dev families) holds on large tables (H1/H2 0.94/0.79 on repo tables) but collapses on small crate tables (0.44-0.47; 69% of crate tables have <10 numeric cells) and on multi-number "and"-lists (0.00-0.03); it emits ~16 false facts per 1000 numbers on non-table prose.
- (k3) Span re-parse catches 100% of value-vs-own-span errors (scale, sign, transposition, decimal) at ~0 false flags; nothing in this stack catches wrong-cell / blank-cell / wrapped-label / side-by-side / transposed mis-assignment (silent 7-85% of extracted facts) or OCR digit-to-digit swaps; two renderings from the SAME extractor agree-on-wrong 100% (cross-source only helps when the surfaces differ: catch 0.448).
- (k3) Footing is almost never applicable on real tables (10 of 175 crate tables, 0 of 74 repo tables) and real totals are rounded (`~15s`) or non-additive: strict 1% footing flagged 3/10, precision-aware 1/10. Plausibility rules built on other real structure false-flag a lot (copyright year stale in 35% of crates, changelog dates non-monotone in 26%, DD/MM dates ambiguous 11.6%): measure the false-flag rate of every rule on real files before using it.
- (k3) Structure-confidence abstention (tau 0.5) is a loss: F1 -0.10/-0.08/-0.02 (dev/held/confirm) for +0.003..0.018 precision; ECE 0.16-0.24. Random 15% "verifier" is not harmless (-0.046 on crates at 0.72 cost).
- (k3) Extraction attempts on layouts the extractor cannot parse cost 1.14-1.17x a plain read (fallback) and answer no better than plain; the cheap-exact-arithmetic gain is concentrated in many-operand questions (col sum/mean .55 -> .90) and vanishes for a strong reader (x0.05 noise) unless verified+repaired.
- (k3) Method traps seen: (i) gold must share unit semantics with the verifier (gold lacked `minutes`; 'wrong' facts were really gold bugs); (ii) footing needs displayed-precision tolerance; (iii) eu-separator evidence must be document-global not extractor-local; (iv) a unit list written after a census of all splits contaminates the transfer test, so only the dev-only extractor (v1) is a clean transfer arm; (v) bootstrap p-values with B=2000 floor at 5e-4 and make a global Holm impossible: use normal-approx p from the bootstrap SE; (vi) ws tables with thousands commas collide with a csv detector (F1 -0.05..-0.08).
- (k3) Real finance/economics text does not exist on this disk (no statements/filings/prices/earnings; 123 currency amounts, ~2800 %, ~1700 sizes, ~2150 timings in markdown): extraction-verification for finance is untested on real finance documents. All k3 numbers are synthetic-until-validated: renderings and prose families are author-written perturbations of real grids; the plain reader is an assumed noise model.
