- (R9-A h17, real vs hash embeddings) The shipped HashEmbedding is NOT a BM25-like stand-in: on real docs its dense+spell MRR is .064 vs MiniLM .292 and BM25 .284 (g1: .591 vs .950 vs .901); its tuned hybrid collapses to BM25 (alpha .3 real, .1 g1; hybrid - BM25 within +-.002). Hash worlds validate plumbing, not retrieval science.
- Flips between hash and MiniLM: dense vs BM25 (tie -> BM25 +.12..+.31 MRR), hybrid adds over BM25 (+.048 -> 0), g1 hybrid F1@3 (-.07 -> +.12), adaptive-k vs fixed-k and exact-dup suppression at k=2 (both reverse/vanish; thresholds are in MiniLM cosine units), end-to-end retrieval gain in topic-dependent worlds (+.022* -> -.002 n.s.). CompactRouter u16/q16 vs f64 (TOST +-.005, 8 worlds, anchors from any encoder, incl. open-vocabulary kd:<doc> features) does NOT flip: 54/54 cells equivalent, worst |diff| .0017.
- Always check tuned hyper-parameters for grid-edge picks per encoder (hash optima sat at alpha .1-.3, drop .02, floors 0.0-0.2): extend the grid before calling a flip, and say when the hash optimum is still at the edge.
- Whitespace/case-sensitive hashing makes spell correction HARMFUL (-.17 MRR on g1 bridge) because corrected text is lowercased; lowercase-word hashing makes it +.03..+.13. Encoder tokenisation is part of the claim.
